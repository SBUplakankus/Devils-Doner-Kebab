---

## 🧠 About the Game

**Devil’s Doner Kebab** is a short, **branching horror story** that puts you in the seat of an *ordinary man* stumbling into a late-night kebab shop after a night of heavy drinking…  

What begins as a harmless visit soon spirals into **a nightmare you’ll never forget.**

> 🍢 *Based on a true story.*  

Make critical dialogue choices that shape the outcome of your night — with **five unique endings**, each revealing a new piece of the story’s mystery.
